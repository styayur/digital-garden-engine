# Building a garden

A digital garden is a place for work that changes. Published pages can be revised, linked and allowed to mature.

![A simple leaf illustration](/garden-assets/leaf.svg)

This example lives in `examples/content/published/posts`. Drafts and private notes are staged by the publication gate only when their status and directory both allow it.
