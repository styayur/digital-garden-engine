# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, trolling, publishing private information without permission, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions or temporarily or permanently ban participation.
